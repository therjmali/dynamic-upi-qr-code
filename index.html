How it works & privacy

Credit Card QR: several banks give every credit card a UPI address (Axis, ICICI, SBI Card, IDFC FIRST, AU, American Express). Pick your bank, enter the details it needs, and the QR lets anyone pay your card bill from a UPI app. Formats come from the banks and cardholder communities and can change, so always send a small test payment first. Card numbers are used only on your device and are never saved.

The QR encodes a standard UPI payment link (upi://pay) with your payee address, name, optional amount and note. Anyone can scan it with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, WhatsApp or any bank UPI app. Everything is generated locally in your browser – no server, no tracking, no signup. Always scan and verify the payee name shown by your UPI app before paying.
